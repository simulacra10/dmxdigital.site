What most small businesses actually need online.

Before a customer reaches out, they want to know three things: what you do, where you work, and whether your business feels dependable. A clear website answers those questions without making them work for it. It gives people one simple place to understand your services, contact you directly, and take the next step with confidence.

What the starter package includes.

  • A focused homepage built around your service, location, and contact path
  • Mobile-friendly layout -- most local searches happen on a phone
  • Click-to-call and direct contact options so customers can reach you without hunting
  • Search metadata so Google can find and describe your business correctly
  • Fast static files you own outright -- no platform subscription holding your site hostage
  • A foundation you can add to over time without rebuilding from scratch

Pricing.

$100 per month

Who this is for.

Most of the businesses I talk to already know they need a better web presence. They just have not had a clear, low-friction way to start. This package is for the business that serves local customers, wants something more professional than a Facebook page, and does not want to hand over thousands of dollars before they have seen a single result. If you want to start with a solid foundation and build on it only when it makes sense, that is exactly what this is designed for.

How it works.

1. Define the offer.

We identify your main service, your ideal visitor, your service area, and the action you want people to take.

2. Build the message.
