The Rule of Three

The page has one job, done three ways.

1

Clarity

Say plainly who you are, what you do, where you serve, and who you help.

2

Credibility

Show the trust signals that matter: local identity, proof, photos, testimonials, guarantees, or experience.

3

Contact

Make the next step obvious with click-to-call, email, quote request, booking, or a simple intake path.
