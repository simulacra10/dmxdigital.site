Most small business websites fail at the basics. Visitors need to know whether they are in the right place, whether they can trust the business, and what they should do next.

The Rule of Three:
1. Clarity
Say plainly who the business is, what it does, where it serves, and who it helps.

2. Credibility
Show the trust signals that matter: local identity, proof, photos, testimonials, guarantees, or experience.

3. Contact
