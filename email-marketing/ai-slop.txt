Subject: AI Slop

AI has become so pervasive that we now have new terms to describe some of the work it produces, including “AI slop.”

AI slop is low-quality, mass-produced digital content generated by artificial intelligence. You have almost certainly encountered it, even if you did not realize it at the time.

But let me be honest: I kind of like some of the flyers AI produces. They are often well laid out, use excellent copy, and convey exactly what needs to be conveyed.

But that raises a more useful question.

Is the problem really that the flyer was made with AI—or is the problem that so much AI content is created without thought, direction, or purpose?

AI is a tool. Used poorly, it produces slop. Used well, it can help a business communicate more clearly, work more efficiently, and reach customers in ways that were not practical just a few years ago.

So here is the question businesses should be asking:

Do you have an AI strategy for your website?
